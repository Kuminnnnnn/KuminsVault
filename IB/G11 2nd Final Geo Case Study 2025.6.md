# Option A: Fresh Water
## **Structural Flood Mitigation Case**
**Three Gorges Dam, Yangtze River, China (2016)**
	• **Goal**: Block flood peaks, protect downstream cities.
	• **Key Data**:
	• Flood storage capacity: 22.15 billion m³.
	• Peak flow reduction: Cut 2016 flood peak from 70,000 m³/s to 42,000 m³/s (-40%).
	• Cost: ¥203 billion (includes relocation).
	• **Results**:
	• **Success**: Lowered Wuhan water levels by 2–3 meters; zero dam breaches in 2016; protected 150 million people.
	• **Issues**: Annual sediment buildup (160 million tons), ecological damage (e.g., endangered Yangtze sturgeon).

## **Non-Structural Flood Mitigation Contrasting Case**
**Case 1: Bangladesh Community Warning Network (2020)**
	• **Goal**: Reduce flood deaths.
	• **Key Data**:
	• 150 monitoring stations + 50,000 volunteers.
	• Alerts: SMS, loudspeakers, boats.
	• **Results**:
	• **Success**: Flood deaths dropped from 138,000 (1991) to <200 (2020).
	• **Why**: Local trust + simple tech (e.g., monsoon calendars).
**Case 2: Bihar, India Warning System (2020)**
	• **Goal**: Early evacuations.
	• **Key Data**:
	• Satellite data + government app.
	• Coverage: 50% flood-prone areas.
	• **Results**:
	• **Failure**: 900 deaths in 2020; only 40% evacuated.
	• **Why**: Tech gaps (15% smartphone use) + slow bureaucracy.

## **Wetland Restroration Case**
**Chongming Dongtan Spartina Control & Habitat Restoration​**
​**核心成果 Key Outcomes​**
- ​互花米草清除​：覆盖率从90%降至15%（2013–2023）。  
    ​Spartina removal: Coverage reduced from 90% to 15%.
- ​水鸟种群​：从5万只增至18万只，黑脸琵鹭越冬数量从15只增至120只。  
    ​Waterbirds: Population surged from 50,000 to 180,000; Black-faced Spoonbill wintering count from 15 to 120.
- ​生态恢复​：底栖生物量+300%，盐沼植被恢复至65%。  
​**未来展望 Future Possibilities​**
1. ​智能湿地管理​
	1. ​AI+无人机监测​：实时追踪入侵物种与鸟类行为，预警生态风险。  
	    ​AI & drones: Real-time monitoring of invasives and bird migration.
	2. ​碳汇交易​：25 km²恢复湿地年固碳1.2万吨，探索碳信用收益反哺保护。  
	    ​Carbon credits: Monetize 12,000t CO₂/year sequestration.
2. ​跨迁飞区网络​
	1. 与韩国顺天湾、澳大利亚卡宾湾共享数据，统一保护标准。  
	    ​EAAF network: Data sharing with Suncheon Bay (Korea) and Kakadu (Australia).
3. ​社区驱动修复​
	1. 培训500名居民参与监测与生态旅游，降低管护成本。  
	    ​Community co-management: Engage locals in eco-tourism and monitoring.
4. ​韧性提升​
	1. 至2030年扩展修复至40 km²，抵御海平面上升与风暴潮。  
	    ​Expand to 40 km²: Enhance coastal resilience by 2030.
​**挑战 Challenges​**
- ​种子库复发风险​：需至少5年持续监测（种子存活期达5年）。  
    ​Seedbank resilience: 5+ years of monitoring required.
- ​资金可持续性​：总投资3.2亿元，需拓展绿色金融（如蓝色债券）。  
    ​Funding: ¥320 million initial cost; need blue finance mechanisms.

## **Water Resources Conflict**
**底格里斯-幼发拉底河冲突核心数据 Tigris-Euphrates Conflict: Key Facts​**

| ​指标 Metric​          | ​数据 Data​                                                                                 |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ​河流长度​               | 幼发拉底河：2,800公里；底格里斯河：1,900公里（西亚第二长河）。                                                      |
| River length         | Euphrates: 2,800 km; Tigris: 1,900 km (2nd longest in West Asia).                         |
| ​依赖人口​               | 超6,000万人依赖幼发拉底河；伊拉克80%用水来自两河。                                                             |
| Dependent population | >60 million rely on Euphrates; 80% of Iraq’s water from both rivers.                      |
| ​流量削减​               | 土耳其水坝使伊拉克供水量减少80%（1975至今）；2021年两河流量分别下降29%（底格里斯）和73%（幼发拉底）。                               |
| Flow reduction       | Turkish dams cut Iraq’s water by 80% since 1975; 2021 flows: -29% Tigris, -73% Euphrates. |
| ​气候影响​               | 流域升温速度为全球两倍；预测2100年两河流量再减30%（幼发拉底）和60%（底格里斯）。                                             |
| Climate impact       | Warming at ​2× global rate; projected flow loss by 2100: 30% Euphrates, 60% Tigris.       |
**冲突根源与激化因素 Root Causes & Escalation​**
1. ​土耳其的水电霸权​：
	1. ​东南安纳托利亚工程（GAP）​​：22座水坝+19电站，控制两河上游（如伊利苏水坝使底格里斯入伊流量减56%）。  
	    ​Turkey’s hydropower hegemony: GAP Project’s 22 dams (e.g., Ilısu Dam cuts Tigris flow to Iraq by 56%).
	2. 能源需求：土耳其60%能源依赖进口，水电占其发电量40%。  
	    ​Energy needs: Hydropower covers 40% of Turkey’s electricity (60% energy imports).
2. ​叙利亚与伊拉克的脆弱性​：
	1. 叙利亚Tishrin大坝2023年仅限生活用水（农业停供）；伊拉克80%农田面临盐碱化。  
	    ​Vulnerability: Syria’s Tishrin Dam restricts water to households; Iraq’s 80% farmland salinized.
3. ​零协议困境​：流域国间无水资源共享条约，全凭临时谈判。  
    ​No treaties: Ad-hoc negotiations replace binding agreements.
**气候变化的催化作用 Climate Change as a Threat Multiplier​**
- ​干旱频率​：2020-2021年为40年来第二干旱年份，叙利亚降雨量低于均值50%。  
    ​Droughts: 2020-21 was 2nd driest in 40 years; Syria’s rainfall -50% below average.
- ​蒸发加剧​：预计流域蒸发量上升20%，进一步削减可用水量。  
    ​Evaporation: Projected +20% evaporation reduces usable water.
