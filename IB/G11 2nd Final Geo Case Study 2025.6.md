# Option A: Fresh Water
## Structural Flood Mitigation Case
**Three Gorges Dam, Yangtze River, China (2016)**
	• **Goal**: Block flood peaks, protect downstream cities.
	• **Key Data**:
	• Flood storage capacity: 22.15 billion m³.
	• Peak flow reduction: Cut 2016 flood peak from 70,000 m³/s to 42,000 m³/s (-40%).
	• Cost: ¥203 billion (includes relocation).
	• **Results**:
	• **Success**: Lowered Wuhan water levels by 2–3 meters; zero dam breaches in 2016; protected 150 million people.
	• **Issues**: Annual sediment buildup (160 million tons), ecological damage (e.g., endangered Yangtze sturgeon).

## Non-Structural Flood Mitigation Contrasting Case
**Case 1: Bangladesh Community Warning Network (2020)**
	• **Goal**: Reduce flood deaths.
	• **Key Data**:
	• 150 monitoring stations + 50,000 volunteers.
	• Alerts: SMS, loudspeakers, boats.
	• **Results**:
	• **Success**: Flood deaths dropped from 138,000 (1991) to <200 (2020).
	• **Why**: Local trust + simple tech (e.g., monsoon calendars).
**Case 2: Bihar, India Warning System (2020)**
	• **Goal**: Early evacuations.
	• **Key Data**:
	• Satellite data + government app.
	• Coverage: 50% flood-prone areas.
	• **Results**:
	• **Failure**: 900 deaths in 2020; only 40% evacuated.
	• **Why**: Tech gaps (15% smartphone use) + slow bureaucracy.

## Wetland Restroration Case
**Chongming Dongtan Spartina Control & Habitat Restoration​**
​**核心成果 Key Outcomes​**
- ​互花米草清除​：覆盖率从90%降至15%（2013–2023）。  
    ​Spartina removal: Coverage reduced from 90% to 15%.
- ​水鸟种群​：从5万只增至18万只，黑脸琵鹭越冬数量从15只增至120只。  
    ​Waterbirds: Population surged from 50,000 to 180,000; Black-faced Spoonbill wintering count from 15 to 120.
- ​生态恢复​：底栖生物量+300%，盐沼植被恢复至65%。  
​**未来展望 Future Possibilities​**
1. ​智能湿地管理​

2. ​AI+无人机监测​：实时追踪入侵物种与鸟类行为，预警生态风险。  
    ​AI & drones: Real-time monitoring of invasives and bird migration.
3. ​碳汇交易​：25 km²恢复湿地年固碳1.2万吨，探索碳信用收益反哺保护。  
    ​Carbon credits: Monetize 12,000t CO₂/year sequestration.

4. ​跨迁飞区网络​

5. 与韩国顺天湾、澳大利亚卡宾湾共享数据，统一保护标准。  
    ​EAAF network: Data sharing with Suncheon Bay (Korea) and Kakadu (Australia).

6. ​社区驱动修复​

7. 培训500名居民参与监测与生态旅游，降低管护成本。

9. 至2030年扩展修复至40 km²，抵御海平面上升与风暴潮。
