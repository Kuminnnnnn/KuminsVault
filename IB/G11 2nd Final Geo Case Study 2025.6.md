# Option A: Fresh Water
## Structural Flood Mitigation Case
**Case 1: Three Gorges Dam, Yangtze River, China (2016)**

• **Goal**: Block flood peaks, protect downstream cities.

• **Key Data**:

• Flood storage capacity: 22.15 billion m³.

• Peak flow reduction: Cut 2016 flood peak from 70,000 m³/s to 42,000 m³/s (-40%).

• Cost: ¥203 billion (includes relocation).

• **Results**:

• **Success**: Lowered Wuhan water levels by 2–3 meters; zero dam breaches in 2016; protected 150 million people.

• **Issues**: Annual sediment buildup (160 million tons), ecological damage (e.g., endangered Yangtze sturgeon).
