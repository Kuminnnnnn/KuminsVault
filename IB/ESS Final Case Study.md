## Unsustainable management of natural resources
Case study: *Guano on Nauru*
- Naura is a small island country in the Pacific ocean, it is home to various birds (stopping point for seagulls)
- The excrement of these birds is called guano, rich nitrogen and phosphorous,  the guano accumulated over years as phosphate reserve.
- In 1900, this phosphate reserve was discovered, the pacific phosphate company made its first sale of phosphate fertilizer in 1907
- The unsustainable traction practices cause the resource to depleted fast
- The country, which was for a time in the 1980s one of the richest in the world was brought to its knees in 2004 with a badly broken economy
## Contrasting water security contry
| 指标      | **Sweden（高水资源安全）**           | **Ethiopia（低水资源安全）**        |
| ------- | ---------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| 水资源安全水平 | 全球最安全国家之一                    | 水资源安全水平低                    |
| 年均降水量   | **≈ 600–1,000 mm/yr**        | **≈ 300–850 mm/yr（高度不稳定）**  |
| 气候条件    | 温带气候，湖泊与河流众多                 | 干旱 / 半干旱地区占比大               |
| 可再生淡水资源 | **≈ 20,000 m³/person/year**  | **≈ 1,200 m³/person/year**  |
| 主要问题    | 局部季节性干旱                      | 长期水资源短缺                     |
| 政策与治理   | EU Water Framework Directive | 国家层面水资源规划有限                 |
| 基础设施    | 先进水处理与管网系统，漏损率低              | 农村地区基础设施不足                  |
| 农业用水    | 高效灌溉（滴灌、精准灌溉）                | 依赖雨养农业                      |
| 气候变化影响  | 可通过技术与管理适应                   | 干旱频率与强度增加                   |
| 政府应对措施  | 长期水资源保护与公众教育                 | One WASH National Programme |
| 饮用水安全   | **>99% 人口可安全饮水**             | **≈ 57% 人口可安全饮水**           |
| 公共意识    | 高水资源保护意识                     | 公共意识有限                      |
| 总体结果    | 水资源供给稳定、可持续                  | 水资源不稳定，区域差异大                |
