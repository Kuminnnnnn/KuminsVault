## Fair trade
global movement to improve the lives of farmers and workers in developing countries by ensuring that they have access to export markets and are paid a fair price for their products
	- better prices
	- decent working conditions
	- fairer deal
**Key guarantees**
1. Prices that aim to cover the average costs of producing their crop sustainably – a vital safety net when market prices drop
2. The Fairtrade Premium – an extra sum of money paid on top of the selling price to invest in business or community projects of their choice
3. Decent working conditions and a ban on discrimination, forced labour and child labour
4. Access to advance credit ahead of harvest time
5. Being able to plan more for the future with more security and stronger relationships with buyers

## Microfinance
- Including extending **small loans, saving and other basic financial services** to people that do not currently have access to capital
- Its a key strategy in helping **people living in poverty** to **become financially independent**
### Benefits of microfinance
1. access
2. better repayment
3. sustainability
4. improving income and end poverty
___
## Social entrepreneurship
- a person who pursues novel applications that have the potential to solve community-based problems
- is interested in starting a business for the greater social good and not just the pursuit of profits
