## UV Radiation
- wavelength is **inversely proportional** to frequency and energy
- **UV** has shorter wavelengths, **higher frequencies and more energy** -> posing greater **danger**
- **UV**:
	- UVA
	- UVB
	- UVC: most dangerous -> blocking by **ozone**
- UV dangers:
	- **immunosuppressive**
	- **mutagenic**
	- **carcinogenic**
	- **may cause skin cancer and cataract**

## Ozone Depletion
1. the amount of ozone in the stratosphere have been reduced over years
2. **Seasonal pattern**: higher in summer than winter -> winter ice clouds allow chemical reactions involving chlorine take place
3. **ODS: ozone depleting substance**
	1. CFC: chlorofluorocarbon
		1. cooling agent -> refrigerators
		2. foamed plastics
		3. aerosols
		4. solvents
	2. HCFC
	3. HFC
	4. Halons
