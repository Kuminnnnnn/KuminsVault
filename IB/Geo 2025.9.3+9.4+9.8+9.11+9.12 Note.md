## Urban Deindustrialization
==is the long-term absolute decline in employment in the manufacturing sectors of an economy.==
**Factors**
	1. the exhaustion of resources
	2. the increasing costs of raw materials, automation and new technology, the introduction of a rival product
	3. fall in demand
	4. overseas competition from NICs
	5. rationalization
	6. rise in costs
	7. the removal of a subsidy
	8. lack of capital

**Impacts**
	1. less government revenue due to less tax
	2. unemployment rate
	3. social stability
	4. GDP reduced, less purchasing power
	5. 

**Positive deindustrialization**: reduce workforce to be more competitive
	mechanization&rationalization
**Negative deindustrialization**: when particular industries decline without any compensating rise in productivity or mechanization
