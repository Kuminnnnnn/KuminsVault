## Human population dynamics
### Population system
1. **Input**
	1. Immigration
	2. New birth
2. **Output**
	1. Emmigration
	2. Death
3. **Indicators to quantify population dynamics**
	1. ==CRB - Crude Birth Rate (‰)：The number of live births per 1000 people in a population per year==
		1. Total number of births/total population * 1000 (‰)
	2. CDR - Crude Death Rate (‰): The number of deaths per 1000 people in a population per year
		1. Total number of dear/total population * 1000 (‰)
