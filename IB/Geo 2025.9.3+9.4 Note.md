## The Process of Urbanization
1. **The Rural Society Phase**
2. **Economic take-off begins**
	1. Rural-urban migration
	2. Most of the immigrants are in their reproductive age -> natural increase
3. **Maturing economy rates of urbanization continue to rise steadily as towns develop and enlarge**
4. **Mass urbanization**
	1. Too much competition
	2. Low opportunity
	3. Decreased life quality
