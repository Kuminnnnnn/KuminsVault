# Land
## Soil
Def: a mixture of mineral particles and organic material that covers the land, and in which terrestrial plants grow
**Fromed by** the weathering of parent rock

**Mineral particles:** sand salt and clay
**Organic material**: living and dead organisms

### Constituents of soil
1. **Rock particles**
	1. Provide **skeleton** of the soil
	2. derived from the underlying **rock** or from rock particles tranported to the environment
2. **Humus**
	1. **plant and animal matter** in the process of decomposition
	2. gives the soil a **dark color**
	3. after breaking down, give back the **mineral nutrient** to the soil
	4. absorb and hold large amount of **water**
3. **Water**
	1. Carry **nutrients**
	2. rapid downward movement caused **leaching**
	3. rapid upward movement cause **salinisation**
	4. large water storage can cause **waterlogging** leading to anoxic conditions and acidification
4. **Air**
	1. provide for **respiration**
5. **Soil organisms**
	1. Mineral **nutrients**
___
### Use of soil
1. providing water, nutrient and anchorage for vegetation
2. providing habitat for decomposers, facilitating carbon and nutrient cycling
3. buffer for temperature change and for the water flow between atmosphere and groundwater
4. pH buffer -> retain nutrient and avoid leaching
___
### Soil formation
1. initial weathering
	1. physical
	2. chemical
	3. biological
2. colonization of activities of organisms -> pioneer species
3. accumulation and decomposition of the organic component
