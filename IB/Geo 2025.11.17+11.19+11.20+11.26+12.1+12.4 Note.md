## MGO
**Trading blocs**: are groups of countries that form trade agreements to reduce or eliminate trade barriers, such as tariffs, among themselves
	**Types of trading blocs**
		1. free trade area
		2. customs union
		3. common market
		4. economic union
	**Influence of trading blocs in global interactions**
	- reduction of protectionism
	- labor flow
	- financial flow
**Free trade zone**: an area within which goods may be landed, handled, manufactured or reconfigured, and reexported without the intervention of the customs authorities
	**EPZs**: export processing zone
