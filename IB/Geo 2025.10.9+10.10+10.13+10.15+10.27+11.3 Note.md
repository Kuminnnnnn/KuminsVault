## Official Development Assistance

ODA flows to countries and territories on the DAC list of ODA Recipients and to multilateral development institutions that are:
1. Provided by:
	1. official agencies (states and governments)
	2. executive agencies
2. Concessional (grants and soft loans) and administered with the promotion of econ development and welfare developing countries as the main objective
