# Globalization
>**Def**: the growing interdependence of countries worldwide through the increasing volume and variety of cross-border transactions in goods and services and of international capital flows, and through the more rapid and widespread diffusion of technology

- Various global interactions
- impacts of global interactions
- Challenges and solutions

**Forms**
1. **Economic**: pushed by the growth of transnational corporations (TNCs)
2. **Social**: the influence of ideas, culture, food, art, sport
3. **Political**: the development and influence of international org on nationstates
___
## How to measure level of globalization
- **A.T. Kearney Index** - 0~1
	- **Based on**
		- **Economic integration**
			- flows of trade
			- foreign direct investments
		- **Personal integration**
			- travel and tourism
			- telephone traffic
			- personal cross-border financial transfers - remittances
		- **Technological connectivity**
			- internet users
			- internet hosts
			- secure servers
		- **Political engagement**
			- intl org
			- UN peacekeeping
			- governmental transfers - intl aids
- **KOF Index** - The Swiss Institute for Business Cycle Research) - 0~100
	- Introduced in 2002
	- **Based on**
		- **economic**
			- flow of trade
			- capital services
			- investments
		- **social**
			- flow of information
			- images
			- culture
			- people across borders
			- TV ownership
		- **political**
			- intl org
			- UN Peace-keeping
### Advantages of KOF/AT Kearney
1. composite indices measure **multidimensional** aspects of globalization
2. quantitative data allow **comparison** over time and among nations
### Disadvantages of KOF/AT Kearney
1. results maybe sometimes driven by extreme **outlying** observations and **missing** values
2. cultural globalization **mostly** refers to the domination of western products
3. some flows of global interactions are **hard** to observe and measure

## Official Development Assistance

ODA flows to countries and territories on the DAC list of ODA Recipients and to multilateral development institutions that are:
1. Provided by:
	1. **official agencies** (states and governments)
	2. executive agencies
2. Concessional (grants and soft loans) and administered with the promotion of **econ development and welfare developing** countries as the main objective
- **Grants**: no need to pay back
- **Sofy loans**: loans with longer repayment period and lower interest

**DAC list**
- Development Assistance Committee -> organisation of OECD
- based on gross national income (GNI) per capita
- low income and mid income
- excluded:
	- G8
	- EU
- Special inclusion: LDCs (least developed countries) defined by UN

## HIPCDRI & MDRI
- **Highly Indebted Poor Country**
	- 1996
	- **HIPC Debt relief Initiative** launched by IMF and World Bank
	- Aiming to ensuring that no poor country **faces a debt burden it cannot manage**
	- **Structural reforms** must be taken
	- Orgs:
		- IMF
		- WB
- **Multilateral Debt Relief Initiative**
	- 2005
	- HIPC is enforced by MDRI to support UNMDGs
	- **100% Debt relief**
	- Orgs
		- IMF
		- WB
		- AfDF
		- IaDB
- **Decision Point**
	- **prerequisit**
		1. be eligible to borrow from **IDA&PRGT**
		2. face **unsustainable debt situation**
		3. **structural reform** under the help of IMF and WB
		4. develop a **PRSP**(Poverty Reduction Strategy Paper)
	- At decision point: **interim relief**
	- after decision point -> **completion point**: **full debt relief**

## FDI
**Lasting interest**: at least 10% voting power in a firm -> lasting interest is established

**Methods of FDI**
1. acquiring voting stock in a foreign company 买股
2. mergers and acquisitions (M&A) 并购
3. joint ventures with foreign corporations 合资
4. starting a subsidiary of a domestic firm in a foreign country 子厂

**Types of FDI**
1. **Horizontal:** a business expands its domestic operations to a foreign country.
2. **Vertical**: a business expands into a foreign country by moving to a different level of the supply chain. 
3. **Conglomerate**: a business acquires an unrelated business in a foreign country.
4. **Platform**: a business expands into a foreign country but the output from the foreign operations is exported to a third country.

**Benefits for investors**
1. expand market
2. cheap labor
3. tax preference
4. subsidies from local government
5. tariffs

**Benefits and costs of host country**
1. access to management expertise, skills, and technology
2. increase in employment
3. economic stimulation

4. displacement of local businesses
5. profit repatriation
6. race to the bottom
7. environmental concerns
