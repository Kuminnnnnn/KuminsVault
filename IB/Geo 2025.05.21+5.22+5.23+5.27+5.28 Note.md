## Pattern of Urban economic activity in cities
- **land users** all compete for the most accessible land within the **CBD**
- **CBD(central business district)**: the commercial and exonomic core of a city
	- the location with the highest land values
	- it has a number of characteristic features and internal zoning
		- Multi-storey development
		- concentration of retaling
		- Public transported is concentrated
		- Offices are concentrated
		- vertical zoning
		- functional segregation
		- few people live in the CBD
		- pedestrain flows are highest
		- traffic restrctions are greatest
		- CBD changes over time
	- **Criterial of CBD**
		- CBHI: total floor area of all CBD function / total groud floor area
		- CBII: total floor area of all CBD funtion / total floor area
		- CBD: CBHI > 1; CBII > 50%
