## Pattern of Urban economic activity in cities
- **land users** all compete for the most accessible land within the **CBD**
- **CBD(central business district)**: the commercial and economic core of a city
	- the location with the highest land values
	- it has a number of characteristic features and internal zoning
		- Multi-storey development
		- concentration of retaling
		- Public transported is concentrated
		- Offices are concentrated
		- vertical zoning
		- functional segregation
		- few people live in the CBD
		- pedestrain flows are highest
		- traffic restrctions are greatest
		- CBD changes over time
	- **Criterial of CBD**
		- CBHI: total floor area of all CBD function / total groud floor area
		- CBII: total floor area of all CBD funtion / total floor area
		- CBD: CBHI > 1; CBII > 50%
	- **Internal structure of CBD**
		- **PLVI**: luxury, high end products
			- only luxury can afford the land price
			- they need this location to demonstrate their brand value
		- **Core**: banks and business
		- **Frame**: wholesalers, car sales, small shops...

## urbanization
1. ==**urbanization**: is the growth of towns and cities, leading to an increasing percentage of the population living in urban area==
2. **suburbanization**&**urban sprawl**
	1. suburbanization -> planned outward expand of city to engulf surrounding village
	2. urban sprawl: unplanned uncontrolled physical expansion
3. **counter-urbanization**
	1. people in the city out-migrate due to high living cost
	2. to commuter village or retire village
4. **re-urbanization**
	1. redevelopment of the city - **City renewal**
	2. innovation on the city service
	3. **Gentrification**: increase the share of wealthy residents and business + improvement on the city facilities -> more attractive
