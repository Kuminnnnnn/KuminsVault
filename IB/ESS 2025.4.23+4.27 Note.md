	3. Industrial discharge

	3. atmosphere
		1. ocean **acidification**
	4. activities at sea
		1. sun screen cream
		2. fisheries
	5. aquaculture farms
		1. excess food
		2. antibiotics
		3. feces

## Plastic pollution in oceans
>**The Great Pacific Garbage Patch**

### *Impact*
1. **Tourism** and **recreation** decreases
2. **entangle** marine animals
3. destroys **habitats** when plastic garbage washed ashore
4. plastic gets inside ship propellers and keels making **ship maintenance more expensive**
5. **Microplastics**
