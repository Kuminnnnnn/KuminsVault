## Climate and weather
> **Climate**: describes how the atmosphere behaves over relatively long periods of time
> **Weather**:  describes the condition in the atmosphere over a short period of time

**Difference**: time scale
**Similarity**: both are affected by ocean and atmospheric circulatory systems

## Mitigation&Adaptation Strategies
### Mitigation
*reduce the causes of climate change*
1. **reduce/stablize GHG**
	1. **agriculture**
		1. CH<sub>4</sub> from live stock
		2. capture CH<sub>4</sub> from landfill site
	2. **carbon tax**
	3. **use renewable energy as alternative of fossil fuel**
	4. **carbon credit**
	5. **energy-efficient applianced**
2. **CO<sub>2</sub> Removal**
	1. **afforestation**
		1. protecting and enhancing carbon sinks
		2. UN-REDD：reduced emission from deforestation and degradation
	2. **Ocean fertilization**
		1. nitrate, phosphate, iron
	3. **Carbon Capture and Storage**
		1. capture CO<sub>2</sub> with chemicals or sorbents
		2. capture CO<sub>2</sub> at site and store in underground
	4. **Biofuel**
		1. sucrane
