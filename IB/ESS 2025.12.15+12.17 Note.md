## Urbanization
Remote rural -> accessble rural -> rural-urban fringe -> suburbs -> inner city -> CBD
### Push factors
1. lack of job opportunities
2. lack of education opportunities
3. limited public transport
4. lack of resources and facility
### Pull factors
1. availability of job
2. education
3. proximity to work
4. convenience
5. easy acess to facilites
### Environmental impact of urbanization
1. habitat loss due to urban expansion
2. loss of agricultural land
3. conversion of land will reduce albedo -> urban heat island
4. decreased infiltration -> increased surface runoff -> river contamination
5. reduced photosynthesis and air purification
6. GHG emission
7. disrupt water cycle due to river channel modification
___
## Suburbanizaiton
### Reasons
- **Improvements in transport** allowed workers to live far away from work place
- **Land was available** on the edges of cities rather than in centers.
- The increase in global trade meant that some farmland could be used for housing developments rather than for food production
- **Better quality** housing, with water, sewers, electricity, gardens and more living space
### Negative impacts
- **loss of natural habitats and reduced biodiversity**
- People commuting to work in the city center might contribute to the **traffic jam and pollution** on roads leading to the city.
- Potential loss of open spaces
### Greenbelt -> preventing suburbanization
Protected natural habitat -> maintain biodiversity

## Urban planning
Aims to meet the **physical, domestic, environmental, commercial, industrial, financial and health** needs of all stakeholders in the community
1. **Exploration phase**: assessing demographics, study land use patterns, identify infrastructure need
2. **Planning phase**: populaiton projection, transportation needs, environmental impact and economic feasibility
3. **Zoning phase**: assign residential, commercial, industrial, and recreational zones
4. **Design and implementation phase**: Design the streetscapes, public spaces, parks, infrastructure systems
5. **Final operational phase**: manage and maintain the infrastructure, public spaces and services of the city
### Garden city & Radiant city
**Garden city**
1. based on neighborhood
2. community
3. small-scale
4. mix of the best of rural and urban
**Radiant city**
5. high rise tower blocks
6. linear city
