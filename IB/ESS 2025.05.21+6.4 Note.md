## Soil Conservation
### Water erosion reduction 
1. **contour farming**
	1. plowing and cultivating along contour lines(perpendicular to the slope)
	2. the furrows trapping water and reduce water erosion
2. **Terracing**
	1. will alter the shape of the slope, unlike **contour**
### Wind reduction
1. **shelter belt**: plant trees between fields
2. **Strip cultivation**: alternate low and high crops in adjacent fields perpendicular to the prevailing wind
### Soil conditioners
1. **lime**
	1. reduce soil acidification
	2. helping clay particles stick together and increase aeration and drainage improvign decomposition
2. **Organic material**
	1. improve texture under soil compaction
	2. supply of nutrients
### cultivation techniques
1. **cover crops**
	1. prevent soil erosion
2. **stop ploughing marginal lands**
3. **no tillage**
4. **avoid overcropping**
	1. deplete soil nutrients
	2. soil erosion
	3. soil compaction
		1. trampling damage leaves
		2. interception reduced
		3. trampling compact soil and damage its structure
		4. soil erosion when bare patches appear
		5. loose soil surface carried away by water and wind
		6. reduced drainage and harder recover of vegetation
5. **Avoid overcropping**
	1. deplete soil nutrients
	2. makes the soil dry and susceptible to wind erosion
### Salinization solution
1. **fushing** the soil and leaching the salt away
2. application of **chemicals**: calcium sulfate to replace the sodium ions and colloids with calcium ions
3. **reduction** in evaporation losses
