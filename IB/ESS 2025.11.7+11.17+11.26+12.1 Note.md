## Natural income
> the yield obtained from natural resources, including goods and services

**Natural resources** refers to **all available resources on earth**, those are beneficial to human are **natural capital**, which will provide **natural income**

**Pros for the concept of natural capital and natural income**
1. lead to more sustainable resource management
	1. sustainable forestry
	2. fishing practices

**Cons**
1. lead to exploitation of nature for human benefit without regard for the intrinsic value of other living things

## The value of natural capital
1. **Use value**
	1. **economic value** - price of markable goods
	2. **technological value** - providing raw material
		1. e.g. Uranium, Lithium
	3. **ecological value** - eco functions
	4. **recreational value** - natural park
2. **Non-use value**
	1. **intrinsic value**
	2. **future use**
	3. **spiritual/cultural value**
___
## Factors affect the value of natural capital
1. **Cultural**
	1. historical events
		1. nuclear disaster
	2. environmental awareness
		1. GHG
	3. spiritual significance, aesthetic point of view
		1. Inuit values whales spiritually
2. **Economic**
	1. valued economically from different perspective
		1. whales was once valued for food but now for tourism
	2. economic development
3. **Technological**
	1. raw material
	2. the development of tech to find new reserves
	3. alternative source
