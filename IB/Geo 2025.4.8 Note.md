#IB 
## Water generated landscape features
___
### River system in deserts
1. Exogenous: Origin out side of the arid environment
2. Endorheic: Flow into the arid environment
3. Ephemeral: Seasonal river
___
### Water generated landforms
1. **Wadi**: is a dry riverbed that contains water during rainy seasons and is located in a valley-like area of the desert
2. **Playa**: flat-bottom depression found in interior desert basins
	- Periodically covered by water
	- Resulting in the deposition of salt, sand and mud
	- Also called salt flat, salt pan
3. **Alluvial fan**: unconsolidated sedimentary deposit that accumulates at the mouth of a mountain canyon
4. **Mesa**: flat-topped mountain or hill. It is wide, flat, elevated landform with steep sides
5. **Buttes**: smaller mesas
___
### Physical characteristics of arid environment
- Lack of water resources
- Large diurnal temperature change
- Strong wind and exposure to sunlight
- Incidence of flash flood
- Remoteness and poor accessibility
