## What is culture?
Culture denotes the systems of shared meaning used by people who belong to the same community, group or nation to help them interpret and make sense of the world
	1. language
	2. customs
	3. beliefs
	4. food
**Changes in cultural traits**
	*language*: 
		- many have lost - ~15 indigenous Australian still being spoken by all age groups, there was ~250 before European came
		- enlarge vocabulary in some languages - English
	**Cultural diffusion**: the spread of cultural ideas from their place of origin to other regions, groups or nations
	**Culture imperialism**: is believed to be resulted from economics forces, as when the dominant culture captures markets for its commodities and thereby gains influence and control over the popular consciousness of other cultures

## Attitudes towards globalization
1. **Hyperglobalists/Capitalists/Free marketeers:** People that are all firmly in favor of a free market system with no or very little government intervention. These groups often blame economic problems on too much intervention. 
2. **Skeptics/Anti-globalists:** People that are opposed to the current system. They may oppose for varying reasons e.g. they want greater government.
	1. envrionmentalism
	2. thrild world debt
	3. animal rights
	4. child labor
	5. anti-capitalism and opposition to multinationals
3. **Transformalists/Reformers:** They accept the existence of the current globalised system, but would like it altered in some way. This maybe greater government regulation, more stringent environmental controls or greater local production.
