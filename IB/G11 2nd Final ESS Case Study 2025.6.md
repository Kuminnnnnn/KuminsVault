# Unit 3
## Invasive Species
- **Grey squirrel Invasion in the UK**
	- **Facts**
		- introduced from North Ameria to UK in the 19th century
		- as ornamental additions
	- **Impacts**
		- outcompete local red squirrels
		- carry the squirrelpox virus -> only kills red squirrels
	- **Management strategies**
		- culling programs
		- forest management: create environment that favors red squirrels - trees selecting
		- research and monitoring
