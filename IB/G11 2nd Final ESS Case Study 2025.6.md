# Unit 3
## Invasive Species
- **Grey squirrel Invasion in the UK**
	- **Facts**
		- introduced from North Ameria to UK in the 19th century
		- as ornamental additions
	- **Impacts**
		- outcompete local red squirrels
		- carry the squirrelpox virus -> only kills red squirrels
	- **Management strategies**
		- culling programs
		- forest management: create environment that favors red squirrels - trees selecting
		- research and monitoring
## IUCN Redlist Species
- **Extinct - Passenger pigeon**
	- time: early 20th century
	- **reasons**:
		- hunting for meat
		- human destruction to the forest habitat
		- low reproduction potential
- **Critically endangered - Sumatran Orangutan**
	- location: indonesian island
	- number: 14000
	- **reasons**:
		- hunting as pet
		- destruction to forest as logging
		- low reproduction potential
- **Improving - Southern White Rhino**
	- **reasons for danger**
		- hunting for horn
	- **strategies**
		- captive breeding programme
		- protected area
		- law enforcement -> **CITES**
	- current number: 18000
