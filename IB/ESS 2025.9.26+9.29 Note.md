# 6.1 Introduction to atmosphere
## Atmosphere system
- the boundary between earth and space
- dynamic system
> **Atmosphere**: an envelope of gas that surrounds the earth, becoming increasingly thinner with distance from the earth's surface and held in place by the earth's gravitational pull

**Air composition**
- **Storage**
	- Dry air
		1. nitrogen (78%)
		2. oxygen (20%)
		3. argon (0.9%)
		4. trace gases (0.01%)
	- Liquid
		1. water droplets in cloud
	- Solid
		1. dust: natural resources debris
		2. ash: completely burnt inorganic matter
		3. soot: incompletely burnt carbon debris
- **Transfer**
	- movement of gas by wind
- **Transformations**
	- condensation of water vapor at atmosphere into water droplets in air
- **Inputs**
	- energy from the sun
- **Output**
	- precipitation

## Global Dimming
>Refers to a reduction in global temperature as a result of pollution

**Natural cause**: volcanic ash, dust storms
**Human activity**: particulate pollution
	**black carbon**
		Direct: absorb insolation due to dark color
		Semi-direct: provide crystal to facillitate cloud formation
		Snow and ice albedo effect: deposit on snow -> reduce albedo -> temp up -> reduce snow cover
		Indirect: black carbon can cause indirect changes in absorption or reflection of solar radiation through changes in cloud cover
