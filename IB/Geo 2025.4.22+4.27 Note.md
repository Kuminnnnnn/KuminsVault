# Glacier system
> the relationship between **total accumulation(input)** and **total ablation(output)**
## Glacier advance
1. Net accumulation of snow - in the **zone of accumulation**
2. Net melting - **Ablation** - in the **zone of ablation**
	- due to low **altitude** 
	- low **temp** - melting > accumulation
3. Melting and iceberg calving

- May be caused by **global dimming**

## Glacier retreat
- May  be caused by **global warming or greenhouse effect**

# Glacial Erosion
## Process
- ==**Abrasion**: the debris carried by the glacier scrapes and scratches the rock==
- ==**Plucking**: Mostly effective in jointed rocks or those weakened by freeze-thaw. As the ice moves, meltwater seeps into the joints and freezes to the rock, which is then ripped out by the moving==
## Landscape
### Group 1:
- **Cirque/Corrie**
- **Arete**: is the sharp-edge ridge formed when tow cirques erode back towards each other
- **Pyramidal Peak**
### Group 2:
- **Trough/u-shaped valley**
	- 1. Glaciers cut distinctive U-shaped valleys with a flat floor and steep sides
	- 2. The glaciers widens, steepens and smoothens V-shaped river valleys
	- **Ribbon lake** usually found in a glacial trough
- **Hanging valley**：are formed by tributary glaciers which do not cut down to the level of the main valley but are left suspended above
- **Sheepback**: formed by the passing of glacier
	- Create asymmetric erosional landform
	- Stoss side: abrasion
	- Leeside: freeze-thaw process (plucking)
