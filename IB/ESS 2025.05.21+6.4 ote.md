## Soil Conservation
### Water erosion reduction 
1. **contour farming**
	1. plowing and cultivating along contour lines(perpendicular to the slope)
	2. the furrows trapping water and reduce water erosion
2. **Terracing**
	1. will alter the shape of the slope, unlike **contour**
### Wind reduction
1. **shelter belt**: plant trees between fields
2. **Strip cultivation**: alternate low and high crops in adjacent fields perpendicular to the prevailing wind
### Soil conditioners
1. **lime** - soil acidification
2. **Organic material** - improve texture under soil compaction
	1. supply of nutrients
### cultivation techniques
1. **cover crops**
	1. prevent soil erosion
2. stop ploughing
3. no tillage
4. avoid overcropping
	1. deplete soil nutrients
