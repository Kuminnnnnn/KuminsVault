# Opportunities and Challenges for Management in Extreme Environment
## Physical characteristics of extreme cold environment
1. **exposure to cold weather**
2. **strong wind**
3. **steep in high mountain region**
4. **long night hours in polar regions**
5. **remoteness**
> **Inuit**
> 	Diet: high in protein high in fat
> 	Clothes: animals fur, skin

## Main human activities in EE
1. **Agriculture in arid area**
	1. **Opportunities**
		1. Heat and sunlight provide **extended growing season**
		2. **Alluvium deposit** provide nutrient rich soil
	2. **challenges in arid envrionment**
		1. Lack of freshwater
		2. exposure to heat and sunlight
		3. flash flood
		4. infertile soil(salinization)
		5. global climate change
		6. desertification - def -> soil degradadtion
	3. **Meathods**
		1. Desert agriculture
			1. drought tolorence plant(well suited for arid conditions) e.g. Sorghum
		2. Desert agroforestry
			1. growing of crops with the environmental support of trees in desert or arid areas
2. **Mineral extraction** - Resource development
	1. **Periglacial area**
		1. **Social economic benefits**
			1. job opportunities
			2. improve income level of local people
			3. increased tax revenue -> improve living standard
		2. **Costs**
			1. Noise pollution
			2. Waste water
		3. **Challenges**
			1. Unstable basis
			2. ground thawing prohibit road transportation
			3. no food supply
			4. remoteness
	2. **Arid**
		1. **Challenges**
			1. low population density
			2. poor working condition
			3. remoteness and inaccessibility
			4. lack of water resource
		2. **Impact**
			1. erosion
			2. formation of sinkholes
			3. loss of biodiversity
			4. contamination of 
				1. soil
				2. groundwater
				3. surface water
			5. air pollution by open air mine
		3. **Benefits**
			1. provide employment
			2. higher income and maybe better life
3. **Tourism**
	1. **Adventure Tourism**: is a type of niche tourism involving exploration or travel to remote areas, where the traveler should expect the unexpected
		1. Cultural heritage: rock art, antiquities, life style of local people
		2. unique landscape
	2. **Positive impact**
		1. showcase the diversity of the cultural and natural heritage
		2. create job opportunities and reduce unemployment
		3. boom local economy
	3. **Negative impact**
		1. Rock art has been lost due to vandalism
		2. pollution associated with tourism 
		3. damage of traditional value
		4. conflict with local people when competing limited resources

## Desertification
Essence: soil degradation
**Cause**:
1. Overuse of fertilizer
2. deforestation
3. inappropriate irrigation
4. ploughing
5. overgrazing
**Impacts**
	Physical impacts
		1. reduced water availability
		2. soil erosion
	Human impacts
		3. reduced food security
		4. poverty
