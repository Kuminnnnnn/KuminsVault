## Desertification
Essence: soil degradation
**Cause**:
1. Overuse of fertilizer
2. deforestation
3. inappropriate irrigation
4. ploughing
5. overgrazing
**Impacts**
	Physical impacts
		1. reduced water availability
		2. soil erosion
	Human impacts
		3. reduced food security
		4. poverty

3. **Infrastructure** -> expensive
	1. 80 degree pipeline to avoid freezing
	2. Three layer window for insulation
