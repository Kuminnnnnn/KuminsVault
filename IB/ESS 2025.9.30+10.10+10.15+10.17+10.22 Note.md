## Climate and weather
> **Climate**: describes how the atmosphere behaves over relatively long periods of time
> **Weather**:  describes the condition in the atmosphere over a short period of time

**Difference**: time scale
**Similarity**: both are affected by ocean and atmospheric circulatory systems

## Mitigation&Adaptation Strategies
### Mitigation
*reduce the causes of climate change*
1. **reduce/stablize GHG**
	1. **agriculture**
		1. CH<sub>4</sub> from live stock
		2. capture CH<sub>4</sub> from landfill site
	2. **carbon tax**
	3. **use renewable energy as alternative of fossil fuel**
	4. **carbon credit**
	5. **energy-efficient applianced**
2. **CO<sub>2</sub> Removal**
	1. **afforestation**
		1. protecting and enhancing carbon sinks
		2. UN-REDD：reduced emission from deforestation and degradation
	2. **Ocean fertilization**
		1. nitrate, phosphate, iron
	3. **Carbon Capture and Storage**
		1. capture CO<sub>2</sub> with chemicals or sorbents
		2. capture CO<sub>2</sub> at site and store in underground
	4. **Biofuel**
		1. sucrane
### Adaptation
*Manage the impacts of climate change*
*reduce adverse effects, maximize positive effects*
1. **Climate change risks**
	1. **costal flooding**
		1. build flood defences
	2. **spread of water-born disease**
		1. vaccination
	3. **Decreased water supply**
		1. desalinization
		2. rainwater collection
		3. grey water recycling
		4. plant drought resistance plants

## International efforts on climate change
### Conference
1. **IPCC(1988)**: intergovernmental panel on climate change, provide an assessment of state of knowledge on climate change
2. **UNFCC(1992)**: United nations framework convention on climate change, to stabilize greenhouse gas concentrations by reducing GHGs emissions in MEDCs
3. **NAPAs(1992)**
### Agreements
1. **Kyoto Protocol(1997)**
	1. first legally binding commitments
	2. reduce GHGs to 5.2% below 1990 before 2012
	3. run out in 2012
2. **Paris Agreement(2015)**
	1. limit global warming to 2 degree C
