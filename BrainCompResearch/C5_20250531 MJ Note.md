# Techniques for studying the brain function
1. **Brain lesion**
	1. patients with damage to various brain regions - battlefield
	2. Animals
	3. cell death due to diseases
2. **Non-invasive recording**
	1. EEG - electroencephalography
		1. low spacial resolution
		2. better temporal resolution
	2. PET - Positron emission tomography
		1. measures blood flow, sugar etc
		2. can tell which part of the brain is active
	3. MRI - Magnetic Resonance Imaging
		1. water will change when thoughts change
	4. **fMRI - Functional MRI**
		1. measure changes in blood oxygenation level
		2. BOLD (Blood-oxygen-level dependent contrast)
		3. low temporal resolution
		4. good spacial resolution
	5. fNIRS - Functional near infrared spectroscopy
