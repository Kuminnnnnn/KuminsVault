# Urban Environment
**Urban area**:
1. minimum population size
2. administrative decision
3. sectoral employment
4. provision of infrastructure and services
___
**stie**: the site of a settlement is the actual land on which a settlement is built, whereas its situation or position refers to its relationship with its surrounding area.

**site factors**:
1. landforms
2. climate
3. vegetation
4. availability of water
5. soil quality
6. mineral
7. wildlife

**area situation factors**:
1. accessibility
2. connection with other locations
3. distance from raw material
