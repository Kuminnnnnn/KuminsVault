
- ​**Principle**:  
    Identifies open chromatin regions (unoccupied by nucleosomes) using the Tn5 transposase enzyme, followed by sequencing to map regulatory elements (e.g., enhancers, promoters).
- ​**Applications**:
    - Epigenetic regulation studies (e.g., chromatin dynamics during development).
    - Screening disease-associated regulatory elements.
- ​**Pros and Cons**:
    - ✅ Fast, sensitive, and requires minimal cells.
    - ❌ Only provides chromatin accessibility data; functional validation needed.
