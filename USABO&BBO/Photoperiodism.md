#### ​**Core Concepts of Photoperiodism**
1. ​**Definition**:
    - ​**Photoperiodism**: A physiological response (e.g., flowering) triggered by specific durations of light or darkness.
    - ​**Critical Night Length**: The minimum or maximum uninterrupted dark period required to induce flowering.
        - ​**Short-Day Plants (SDPs)**: Require a ​**long night** (≥ critical night length, e.g., 8 hours for cocklebur).
        - ​**Long-Day Plants (LDPs)**: Require a ​**short night** (≤ critical night length).
___
1. ​**Plant Classification**:
    - ​**Short-Day Plants (SDPs)**:
        - Flower only when nights exceed a critical duration (e.g., cocklebur _Xanthium strumarium_ requires ≥8 hours of darkness).
        - Misnamed as "short-day" plants; they are ​**long-night plants**.
    - ​**Long-Day Plants (LDPs)**:
        - Flower when nights are shorter than a critical duration.
        - More accurately termed ​**short-night plants**.
    - ​**Day-Neutral Plants**: Flower regardless of night/day length.
