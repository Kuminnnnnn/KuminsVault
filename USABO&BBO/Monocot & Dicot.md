### ​**Monocotyledons (Monocots)**

**Characteristics**: Single cotyledon, parallel leaf venation, scattered vascular bundles, fibrous root system, floral parts typically in multiples of three (e.g., lilies, corn, wheat).  
**Common Families/Genera**: Poaceae (e.g., corn, wheat), Amaryllidaceae (e.g., garlic, onion), Orchidaceae, etc.

>玉米、百合、大蒜、洋葱、谷物
### ​**Dicotyledons (Dicots)**

**Characteristics**: Two cotyledons, net-like (reticulate) leaf venation, ring-shaped vascular bundles, taproot system, floral parts typically in multiples of four or five (e.g., legumes, roses, poppies).  
**Common Families/Genera**: Papaveraceae (e.g., poppy), Brassicaceae, Fabaceae, etc.

> 豆类、玫瑰、罂粟、花卉、根茎作物
