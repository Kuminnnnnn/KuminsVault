
## Intercellular Communication Classification

 - **Endocrine signaling**: through blood vessel to remote tissues
 - **Paracrine signaling**: secreted molecules diffuse locally to neighboring cells
 - **Autocrine signaling**: diffuse locally to itself
 - **Synaptic signaling**: neurotransmitter diffuse across synapses to target tissue
 - **Neuroendocrine signaling**: neurohormone diffuse into the bloodstream to remote cells

>*Pheromones*: chemicals secrete into the air that are used to communicate with other animals

---
## Classes of Hormones

- **Polypeptide**: e.g. Insulin
	- Water soluble - bind with receptor on the cell membrane
- **Steroid**: e.g.  [[Cortisol]]
	- Lipid soluble - bind with receptor in the cytoplasm or nucleus
- **Amines**: e.g. [[Epinephrine]] 
	- lipid soluble: Thyroid Hormone, [[Melatonin]]
	- Water soluble: Epinephrine
