### **Ammonia**
- **Toxic** when high concentration
- Requires **large** amount of water to maintain a low concentration
- Most often in **aquatic** species
- Can **interconvert** between **NH<sub>3</sub> and NH<sub>4</sub><sup>+</sup>** 
- Can easily **diffuse** into surrounding environment **through skin**
___
### **Urea**
- Synthesis though combining **ammonia** with **carbon dioxide** by **ATP(energy cost)**
- Very **low toxicity**
___
### **Uric Acid**
- **Non-toxic**
- **Insoluble**
- **Semi-solid** state with **low** water loss
- Requires even **more energy** to synthesis from **ammonia**
