### **Core Functions and Mechanisms of Toll-like Receptors (TLRs)**

**Toll-like receptors (TLRs)** are key ​**pattern recognition receptors (PRRs)** in the innate immune system. They recognize ​**pathogen-associated molecular patterns (PAMPs)** and ​**damage-associated molecular patterns (DAMPs)**, triggering immune responses to eliminate infections. Below is a detailed breakdown of their functions, classification, and mechanisms:

---

#### ​**1. Core Functions of TLRs**

1. ​**Pathogen Recognition**:
    
    - ​**Targets**:
        - ​**Pathogen-associated molecular patterns (PAMPs)**:  
            Examples: Bacterial lipopolysaccharide (LPS), viral double-stranded RNA (dsRNA), fungal β-glucans.
        - ​**Damage-associated molecular patterns (DAMPs)**:  
            Examples: Host-derived nucleic acids (released during cell damage), heat shock proteins (HSPs).
    - ​**Specificity**: Each TLR recognizes distinct PAMPs (e.g., TLR4 detects LPS; TLR3 detects dsRNA).
2. ​**Immune Activation**:
    
    - ​**Trigger inflammatory responses**: Activate ​**NF-κB** and ​**interferon regulatory factors (IRFs)**, leading to the release of ​**pro-inflammatory cytokines** (e.g., IL-1β, TNF-α) and ​**type I interferons** (IFN-α/β).
    - ​**Bridge innate and adaptive immunity**: Promote dendritic cell maturation, enhance antigen presentation, and activate T/B lymphocytes.

---

#### ​**2. Classification and Localization of TLRs**

|​**TLR Type**|​**Localization**|​**Ligands Recognized**|​**Pathogen Targets**|
|---|---|---|---|
|​**TLR1/2**|Plasma membrane|Bacterial lipoproteins (Gram-positive bacteria)|Bacteria, fungi|
|​**TLR3**|Endosomal membrane|Viral double-stranded RNA (dsRNA)|RNA viruses (e.g., influenza)|
|​**TLR4**|Plasma membrane|Lipopolysaccharide (LPS) of Gram-negative bacteria|Gram-negative bacteria (e.g., _E. coli_)|
|​**TLR5**|Plasma membrane|Bacterial flagellin|Flagellated bacteria (e.g., _Salmonella_)|
|​**TLR7/8**|Endosomal membrane|Viral single-stranded RNA (ssRNA)|RNA viruses (e.g., HIV, dengue)|
|​**TLR9**|Endosomal membrane|Unmethylated CpG DNA|DNA viruses, bacteria (e.g., herpesviruses, _M. tuberculosis_)|

---

#### ​**3. TLR Signaling Pathways**

1. ​**MyD88-Dependent Pathway** (used by most TLRs):
    
    - ​**Steps**:
        1. TLR binds ligand → recruits adaptor protein ​**MyD88** → activates IRAK kinases → phosphorylation cascade.
        2. Activates ​**NF-κB** and ​**MAPK pathways** → releases pro-inflammatory cytokines (e.g., IL-6, TNF-α).
    - ​**TLRs involved**: TLR1/2/4/5/6/7/8/9.
2. ​**TRIF-Dependent Pathway** (used by TLR3/4):
    
    - ​**Steps**:
        1. TLR3/4 activation → recruits adaptor protein ​**TRIF** → activates TBK1 kinase.
        2. Phosphorylates ​**IRF3** → induces ​**type I interferon (IFN-β)** expression → enhances antiviral immunity.

---

### ​**Key Takeaways**

- TLRs serve as sentinels of innate immunity, detecting microbial components via PAMPs/DAMPs.
- Their signaling pathways drive inflammation and interferon responses, crucial for pathogen clearance.
- Dysregulation of TLRs is linked to autoimmune diseases, chronic inflammation, and cancer.
