## **Key Gene Classes and Their Roles**

1. ​**Class A Genes**
    
    - ​**Function**: Specify ​**sepals** (outermost whorl).
    - ​**Examples**: _APETALA1 (AP1)_, _APETALA2 (AP2)_.
    - ​**Mechanism**: A genes are active in the first whorl and suppress Class C genes in outer regions of the floral meristem.
2. ​**Class B Genes**
    
    - ​**Function**: Specify ​**petals** (second whorl) and contribute to ​**stamens** (third whorl).
    - ​**Examples**: _APETALA3 (AP3)_, _PISTILLATA (PI)_.
    - ​**Mechanism**: B genes require co-expression with A genes for petal formation and with C genes for stamen development.
3. ​**Class C Genes**
    
    - ​**Function**: Specify ​**carpels** (fourth whorl) and contribute to ​**stamens** (third whorl).
    - ​**Examples**: _AGAMOUS (AG)_.
    - ​**Mechanism**: C genes suppress A genes in the inner whorls and are essential for stamen and carpel identity.
4. ​**Class D Genes**
    - ​**Function**: Specify ​**ovules** (within carpels).
    - ​**Examples**: _SEEDSTICK (STK)_, _SHATTERPROOF (SHP)_.
    - ​**Mechanism**: D genes act alongside C genes to promote ovule development within carpels.
5. ​**Class E Genes**
    
    - ​**Function**: ​**Essential co-factors** for all floral organs.
    - ​**Examples**: _SEPALLATA1-4 (SEP1-4)_.
    - ​**Mechanism**: E genes form protein complexes with A, B, C, or D genes to activate organ-specific pathways.

---

#### ​**Combinatorial Gene Activity Determines Floral Organs**

|​**Whorl**|​**Organ**|​**Active Gene Classes**|
|---|---|---|
|1st (Outermost)|Sepals|A + E|
|2nd|Petals|A + B + E|
|3rd|Stamens|B + C + E|
|4th (Innermost)|Carpels|C + E|
|Ovules (in carpels)|Ovules|C + D + E|
