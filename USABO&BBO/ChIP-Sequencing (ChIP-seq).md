
- ​**Principle**:  
    Uses antibodies to enrich DNA fragments bound by specific proteins (e.g., transcription factors, histone modifications), followed by sequencing to map protein-DNA interaction sites.
- ​**Applications**:
    - Transcription factor binding site analysis (e.g., p53 binding during DNA damage).
    - Histone modification profiling (e.g., H3K4me3 marking active promoters).
- ​**Key Dependency**:
    - High-specificity antibodies (e.g., anti-H3K27ac).
- ​**Pros and Cons**:
    - ✅ Directly links proteins to DNA interactions.
    - ❌ Experimentally complex; requires antibody optimization.
