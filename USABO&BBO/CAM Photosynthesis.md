### ​**1. Definition and Naming**

- ​**Full Name**: Crassulacean Acid Metabolism (CAM)
- ​**Origin of Name**: First discovered in plants of the Crassulaceae family (e.g., _Bryophyllum_), characterized by nighttime CO₂ fixation into malic acid.
[[Photosynthesis]]

---

### ​**2. Core Mechanism**

- ​**Temporal Separation Strategy** (contrasting with the _spatial separation_ in C4 plants):
    - ​**Nighttime**: Stomata open to absorb CO₂, which is fixed into ​**malic acid** (stored in vacuoles).
        - Key enzyme: ​**PEP carboxylase** (similar to C4 plants but active only at night).
    - ​**Daytime**: Stomata close, malic acid breaks down to release CO₂ for the Calvin cycle to synthesize sugars.

---

### ​**3. Adaptive Environments**

- ​**Extreme arid regions**: Deserts, rocky crevices (e.g., cacti, agave).
- ​**High diurnal temperature fluctuations**: Reduces water loss via transpiration during cooler nighttime.

---

### ​**4. Representative Species**

- ​**Economic crops**: Pineapple (Bromeliaceae family), aloe.
- ​**Succulents**: Cacti, _Lithops_ (living stones), _Kalanchoe_ (Crassulaceae family).

---

### ​**5. Key Differences from C4 Plants**

| ​**Feature**              | ​**CAM Plants**                              | ​**C4 Plants**                           |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| ​**CO₂ fixation timing**  | Fix at night, utilize during day             | Fix and utilize simultaneously day-round |
| ​**Stomatal activity**    | Open only at night                           | Open during day (may partially close)    |
| ​**Water-use efficiency** | Higher (desert survival)                     | Moderate (tropical savanna/croplands)    |
| ​**Growth rate**          | Extremely slow (limited photosynthetic time) | Faster (efficient spatial division)      |
[[C4 and C3 Plants]]

---

### ​**6. Ecological Significance**

- ​**Survival in extreme conditions**: Thrives in deserts with annual rainfall <200 mm.
- ​**Stress tolerance**: Withstands temperatures up to 50°C and prolonged droughts (e.g., cacti surviving decades without precipitation).

---

### ​**7. Human Applications**

- ​**Arid-region agriculture**: Commercial pineapple cultivation in poor sandy soils.
- ​**Ecological restoration**: CAM plants used in desertification control (e.g., agave planting for sand stabilization).
