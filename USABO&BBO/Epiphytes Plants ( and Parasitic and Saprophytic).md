### ​**Epiphytes**

**Definition**: Plants that grow on the surface of other plants (e.g., tree trunks, branches) ​**without parasitizing them**, using the host solely for physical support. They obtain water and nutrients from the air, rain, or decaying organic matter (humus).

**Characteristics**:

- Do not damage host tissues or compete for nutrients.
- Common in tropical rainforests (e.g., orchids, bromeliads, ferns).

---

### ​**Comparison with Parasitic and Saprophytic Plants**

|​**Plant Type**|​**Nutrient Source**|​**Impact on Host**|
|---|---|---|
|​**Epiphyte**|Air, rainwater, humus|Neutral (no impact)|
|​**Parasitic Plant**|Directly absorbs water/nutrients from host|Harmful (host damaged)|
|​**Saprophytic Plant**|Decomposes dead organic matter (non-photosynthetic)|Does not rely on living hosts|
