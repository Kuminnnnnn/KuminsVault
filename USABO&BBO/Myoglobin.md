**Myoglobin** is a small, globular protein primarily found in ​**skeletal and cardiac muscle tissues**. It plays a critical role in storing and facilitating oxygen diffusion within muscle cells, particularly during high-energy demand or low oxygen conditions (e.g., intense exercise or hypoxia).

---

#### ​**Structure**

1. ​**Single polypeptide chain**: Composed of 153 amino acids.
2. ​**Heme prosthetic group**: Contains an iron-containing porphyrin ring (heme) that binds one oxygen molecule (O₂).
3. ​**3D conformation**: Compact tertiary structure with 8 α-helices, enabling rapid oxygen binding/release.

---

#### ​**Key Functions**

- ​**Oxygen storage**: Acts as an intracellular oxygen reservoir, ensuring steady supply to mitochondria for ATP production.
- ​**Oxygen buffering**: Regulates oxygen levels during muscle contraction when blood flow is restricted.
- ​**Facilitated diffusion**: Enhances O₂ movement from capillaries to mitochondria in muscle cells.

---

#### ​**Myoglobin vs. Hemoglobin**

|​**Feature**|​**Myoglobin**|​**Hemoglobin**|
|---|---|---|
|​**Structure**|Monomeric (single subunit)|Tetrameric (4 subunits: 2α + 2β)|
|​**O₂ affinity**|High (binds O₂ at very low partial pressure)|Lower (cooperative binding)|
|​**O₂ binding curve**|Hyperbolic|Sigmoidal (cooperative)|
|​**Location**|Muscle tissues|Red blood cells|
