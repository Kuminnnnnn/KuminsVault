
- ​**Principle**:  
    Uses a deactivated Cas9 protein (dCas9) fused to transcriptional repressors to target and suppress specific DNA regions (e.g., enhancers), followed by RNA-seq to observe downstream gene expression changes.
- ​**Applications**:
    - Validating functional enhancers/silencers.
    - Decoding gene regulatory networks.
- ​**Workflow**:  
    Design sgRNAs targeting enhancers → Transfect cells → RNA-seq analysis.
- ​**Pros and Cons**:
    - ✅ Causality-based (not correlative); strong functional validation.
    - ❌ Requires sgRNA design and controls for off-target effects.
