## **C<sub>4</sub> Plants**
In arid area
![[C4 Plants.png]]
- **Naming & Mechanism**:
    - Named for producing a ​**4-carbon compound** (oxaloacetate) as the first product of carbon fixation.
    - Uses ​**PEP carboxylase** (high CO₂ affinity) in mesophyll cells to pre-fix CO₂ before the Calvin cycle.
      
- ​**Evolution**:
    - Evolved independently at least ​**45 times** across 19 plant families.
    
- ​**Key Species**:
    - Includes agriculturally vital crops like ​**sugarcane** and ​**corn** (grasses).

- ​**Leaf Anatomy**:
    - Two specialized cell types:
        1. ​**Bundle-sheath cells**: Tightly packed around leaf veins, host Calvin cycle.
        2. ​**Mesophyll cells**: Loosely arranged, adjacent to bundle-sheath cells, perform initial CO₂ fixation.

- ​**Functional Advantage**:
    - Efficient CO₂ concentration mechanism minimizes photorespiration.
    - Thrives in ​**high-temperature**, ​**high-light**, and ​**low-CO₂** environments.
