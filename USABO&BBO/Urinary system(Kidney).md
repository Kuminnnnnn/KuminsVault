### **Regulations**
1. **blood pressure**
- **Higher blood pressure** -> **Contraction** of **Glomerular** -> **Reduce Glomerular Filtration Rate**
1. **Coffey** & **Alcohol**
- **Inhibit** the secretion of **ADH**
	- **ADH**: move **aquaporin** to the **basal** side to **promote** water **reabsorption**
