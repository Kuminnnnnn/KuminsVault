**Enterochromaffin-like cells (ECL cells)** are critical endocrine cells in the stomach, primarily involved in regulating gastric acid secretion. Below are their key features:

---

### ​**1. Definition and Location**

- ​**Definition**:
    - ECL cells belong to the ​**gastrointestinal endocrine cell** family. They are named for their histochemical similarities to enterochromaffin cells (EC cells) but differ in secretory products.
- ​**Location**:
    - Predominantly located in the ​**gastric glands** of the stomach body and fundus, adjacent to parietal cells.[[Stomach Secretion]]

---

### ​**2. Core Functions**

- ​**Histamine Secretion**:
    
    - ECL cells release histamine via ​**paracrine signaling** to stimulate ​**parietal cells**, promoting HCl secretion.
    - ​**Mechanism**: Histamine binds to ​**H₂ receptors** on parietal cells → activates adenylate cyclase → increases cAMP → enhances ​**H⁺/K⁺-ATPase (proton pump)** activity.
- ​**Positive Feedback Regulation**:
    
    - ECL cell activity is controlled by ​**gastrin** (secreted by G cells) and ​**acetylcholine** (released by vagal nerves).
    - Gastrin binds to ​**CCK₂ receptors** on ECL cells → triggers histamine release.

---

### ​**3. Synergy with Other Cells**

- ​**G cells**: Secrete gastrin to stimulate ECL cells.
- ​**Parietal cells**: Secrete HCl under the combined action of histamine, gastrin, and acetylcholine.
- ​**D cells**: Release ​**somatostatin** to inhibit both ECL and G cells, forming a negative feedback loop.

---

### ​**4. Clinical Relevance and Diseases**

1. ​**Zollinger-Ellison Syndrome (Gastrinoma)**:
    
    - Tumor overproduces gastrin → chronic ECL cell activation → excessive gastric acid → peptic ulcers and diarrhea.
2. ​**ECL Cell Hyperplasia and Gastric Carcinoids**:
    
    - ​**Long-term proton pump inhibitor (PPI) use**: Reduces gastric acid → compensatory hypergastrinemia → ECL hyperplasia (rarely progresses to neuroendocrine tumors).
    - ​**Chronic atrophic gastritis**: Low gastric acid → elevated gastrin → ECL hyperplasia.
3. ​**Autoimmune Gastritis**:
    
    - Parietal cell destruction → reduced acid → compensatory gastrin rise → ECL hyperplasia.

---

### ​**5. Differentiation from Enterochromaffin Cells (EC Cells)**

| ​**Feature**           | ​**ECL Cells**                   | ​**EC Cells**                                               |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| ​**Primary Secretion** | Histamine                        | Serotonin (5-HT)                                            |
| ​**Main Distribution** | Stomach body and fundus          | Throughout the GI tract (especially small/large intestines) |
| ​**Function**          | Stimulate gastric acid secretion | Regulate gut motility, secretion, and pain perception       |
