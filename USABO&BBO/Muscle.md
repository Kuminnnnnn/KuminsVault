### Classification
1. **Smooth** **muscle**
	- non-striated
	- stomach, air ways, blood vessels
	- push things
	- involuntarily
2. **Cardiac muscle**
	- straited
	- in-heart
	- involuntarily
3. **Skeletal muscle**
	- straited
	- voluntarily
___

### Proteins
**Myofibril** can be divided into different **Sarcomere肌小节** 
Each sarcomere contains two different **microfilaments**: **Myosin** and **Actin**
*Myosin and actin has different function in cardiac muscle compare to skeletal muscle*
![[Skeletal Muscle.png]]
#### **The sliding filament model of muscle contraction**
**In relaxation**:
Actin and myosin are **not contacted**, but they want to!
Actin are blocked by **tropomyosin** and **troponin**
![[tropomyosin and troponin.png]]
**Sarcoplasmic Reticulum(SR)**: ER in muscle cell
- contains **Ca<sup>+</sup> pump** on it which can **store Ca<sup>+</sup> with ATP**

When Action potential(acetylcholine) travels down along **T tubules**:
![[T tubules.png]]
- trigger the **Voltage-gated Ca<sup>+</sup> channel**
- Ions bind to **troponin** and **tropomyosin**
- Both two complex will **leave** and stop blocking **actin**
___
![[Muscle contraction.png]]
After the contraction, the **Ca**<sup>+</sup> will be recruit back to the **SR**
___
### Muscle Movement
1. **Muscle always pull, never push!!!**
- **Origin**: the non move site of muscle linked to the bone
- **Insertion point**: moving site
2. **What one muscle done, another can always undo it!**
	- **Prime** **movers主动肌**
		- for adduction
	- **Antagonist拮抗肌**
		- for abducting
	- **Synergist协同肌**
		- for aiding
#### **Motor units**
A group of muscle fibers that all get their signals from **the same, single motor neuron**
	**Small unit**: delicate motion
	**Big unit**: large movement
The motion is controlled by the **frequency** of signal - **Graded muscle Responses**
**Twitch** will sum up into greater movement - **Temporal Summation**
- Maximum summation - **Tetanus**
- ![[Tetanus.png]]
- This will consume ATP, finally leading to **muscle fatigue**
****
### The Size Principle
1. First: the **most active single unit** will recruit for contraction
2. Last: the **largest least-active unit** will be recruited
___
### Two types of contraction
1. **Isotonic**: change in length
	- e.g.: pick up a cup
2. **Isometric**: no change in length
	- e.g.: push a building
