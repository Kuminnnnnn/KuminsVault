1. **Meristematic Cells**: The cells plants use to grow
	1. Apical meristem: grow taller or deeper
	2. Lateral meristem: grow thicker
	3. Intercalary meristem: node
2. **Parenchyma Cells**: Do most of the work for the plant
	1. photosynthesis
	2. transportation
	3. storage
	4. structure of fruit
3. **Collenchyma Cells**: Structural support and protection
	1. some of photosynthesis
	2. some of storage
	3. provide flexible structure
4. **Sclerenchyma Cells**: Dead cells in areas no longer growing
	1. woody tissue
	2. fibers
	3. sclereids(shell of fruits)
