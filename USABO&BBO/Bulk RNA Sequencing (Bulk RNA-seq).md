
- ​**Principle**:  
    Uses high-throughput sequencing to analyze the expression levels of all mRNAs in a tissue or cell population.
- ​**Applications**:
    - Screening for differentially expressed genes (e.g., cancer vs. normal tissues).
    - Transcriptome profiling.
- ​**Workflow**:  
    mRNA extraction → Library preparation → Sequencing → Data analysis (e.g., DESeq2 for differential gene identification).
- ​**Pros and Cons**:
    - ✅ Comprehensive transcriptome coverage, ideal for exploratory research.
    - ❌ Cannot resolve cellular heterogeneity (requires single-cell RNA-seq for deeper insights).
