Affected by:
1. **CO<sup>2</sup> concentration**
	>***Bohr effect***: CO₂ + H₂O↔H₂CO₃↔H⁺ + HCO₃⁻
	- Reducing **pH value**
	- H<sup>+</sup> will bind with hemoglobin and induce *conformational change*: from R to T
	- T state of hemoglobin will have <u>Lower Oxygen Affinity</u>
		- Aid releasing oxygen for high demanding sport
2. **Altitude**
	- Synthesis **Erythropoietin (EPO)**
		- produce more RBC
		- increase **oxygen carrying capacity**
		- ***Do NOT*** directly affect **affinity**
3. **Fetal Hemoglobin**
	- HbF: <u>Higher Oxygen Affinity</u>
	- help fetus to grow
4. Hemoglobin with Fe<sup>3+</sup>
	- ***Cannot*** bind with oxygen
