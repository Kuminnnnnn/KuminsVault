## 1. ​**Identity of Florigen**:
- ​**FT Protein (FLOWERING LOCUS T)**: Synthesized in leaf vascular cells, serving as the mobile flowering signal in long-day plants.
___
## 2. ​**Transport Pathway**:
- FT protein travels through ​**plasmodesmata** within the phloem, directly moving from cell to cell.
- ​**Plasmodesmata Function**: Facilitate symplastic transport of macromolecules (proteins, RNA) without extracellular secretion.
