
- ​**Principle**:  
    Uses fluorescently labeled DNA or RNA probes to bind specifically to the mRNA or DNA sequence of a target gene, allowing visualization of its location in cells or tissues under a microscope.
- ​**Applications**:
    - Gene localization (e.g., detecting chromosomal abnormalities).
    - Spatial visualization of mRNA expression (e.g., HER2 detection in tumors).
- ​**Pros and Cons**:
    - ✅ High resolution for precise gene localization.
    - ❌ Dependent on known sequences; unsuitable for discovering novel genes.
