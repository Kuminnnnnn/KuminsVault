### The Core Conflict: Main Parties

- **Elijah**: The prophet of Yahweh, representing orthodox monotheism. He declares a severe drought to punish Israel's apostasy, defeats the pagan prophets with a miracle on Mount Carmel, and receives a new mission from God during his lowest moment at Mount Horeb (Sinai).
    
- **Ahab**: The king of the northern kingdom of Israel. He vigorously promoted pagan worship nationwide, pushing the country into a crisis of faith and morality. He is the quintessential "evil king" in the books of Kings.
    
- **Jezebel**: Ahab's queen, a foreign princess from Sidon. She was a fanatical promoter of Baalism in Israel, massacred the prophets of Yahweh, and threatened to hunt down Elijah after her prophets were defeated at Mount Carmel.
    

### Key Believers and Witnesses

- **The Widow of Zarephath**: A poor, Gentile widow living in Sidon (ironically, Jezebel's homeland and the heartland of Baal worship). She sustained Elijah during the severe drought and witnessed two major miracles: the never-ending supply of flour and oil, and the resurrection of her dead son.
    
- **Obadiah**: The royal palace administrator for King Ahab. Despite serving the enemy, he secretly feared Yahweh and risked his life to hide and feed 100 prophets of Yahweh. In chapter 18, he reluctantly acted as the messenger to arrange the meeting between Elijah and Ahab.
    

### Representatives of Paganism

- **Prophets of Baal and Asherah**: The 450 prophets of Baal and 400 prophets of Asherah supported by Queen Jezebel. They engaged in a "duel by fire" with Elijah on Mount Carmel, failed to evoke a response from their god, and were ultimately executed by Elijah.
    

### The "New Generation" Appointed by God (Chapter 19)

- **Elisha**: The son of a wealthy farmer, Shaphat. He was called by Elijah at the end of chapter 19. After saying goodbye to his parents, he followed Elijah to become his prophetic successor.
    
- **Hazael and Jehu**: Two future kings whom God commanded Elijah to anoint (Hazael as the king of the enemy nation Aram/Syria, and Jehu as the new king of Israel). They were the "political tools" God arranged to eventually wipe out the dynasty of Ahab and Jezebel.
