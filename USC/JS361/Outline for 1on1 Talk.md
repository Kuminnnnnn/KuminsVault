# Urban Heat Island Effect and Its Potential Solution
*Urban Heat Island Effect in the Five New Cities of Shanghai and the pilot "blue-green infrastructure planning" under the Shanghai Climate Change Adaptation Action Plan.*
## Background Info
- **Urban Heat Island Effect**: Urban regions become warmer than their rural surroundings, forming an "island" of higher temperatures in the landscape.
	- Causes:
		- reduced vegetation and water body in urban area -> reduced evapotranspiration
		- properties of urban materials (mostly are impervious)
			- reflectivity
			- absorption
			- releasing
		- urban geometry -> heat might be trapped in the city
		- anthropogenic heat
			- Human
			- AC
			- Cars
## Environmental Impacts
- Nighttime heat retention -> greater cooling demand -> greater energy consumption (AC)
- Increased energy cost -> higher emission of air pollutants
- compromised human health and comfort
- Impaired water quality (thermal pollution)
## Range and Trends
- Five New Cities -> Urban Expansion -> Shanghai's UHI expanded toward suburban areas
- Population heat exposure increased
	- high-risk areas in central Shanghai
	- emerging risks in peripheral suburbs
- Shanghai-wide UHI extent increased during 2000–2015, then declined slightly during 2015–2020
- From 2005 to 2020, daytime UHI intensity decreased in 4/5 Five New Cities except for Nanhui
- Heat patches became increasingly connected -> regional heat-island formation
