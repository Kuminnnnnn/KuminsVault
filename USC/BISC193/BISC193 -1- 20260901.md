# Choosing a lab..
## Topics
- Basic vs. clinical research
- choose a broad field, ont a specific topic
- keep an open mind, dont limit yourself
- consider research in social sciences, humanities, arts
- follow your interests
- you are not expected to devise a project, nor is this desirable
## Sponsors/Mentors
- make a long list of possible sponsors
- get to know teachers, for sponsorship and referrals
- ask advanced students who are involved in research, e.g. TAs
- department websites
- graduate program websites
- other usc schools
- institutes
---
# How to get involved
- get to know your faculty instructors
- go to office hours
- ask about research
- plan ahead, be willing to prepare by reading the lab's publications
- consider a long-term involvement
