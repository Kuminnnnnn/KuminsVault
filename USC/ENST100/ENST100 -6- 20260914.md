# Population and Community Ecology
- **Anthropogenic**: of, relating to, or resulting from the influence of human beings on nature
	- human effects on nature
- **Ecology**: the relationship of organisms to each other and their physical surroundings
- **Population Ecology**: the study of factor that regulate population distribution and abundance
- **Community Ecology**: the study of species interactions
- Rate of growth = birth rate - death rate

## Life History Strategies
- **K-Selected**: stable populations adapted to exsit at or near K of the environment
- **R-Selected**: High rate of per capita population growth, r, but poor competitive ability

## Limitations to population growth
- **Density Dependent Mortality Factors**
	- a factor whose influence varies with the density of the population
- **Density Independent Mortality Factors**
	- not affected by population
---
## Niche
- all the environmental requirement of a species
	- **Biotic Interactions**
		- competition
		- predation
		- herbivory
		- parasitism
		- facilitation
	- **Abiotic**
		- temp
		- soil
		- nutrition
		- light
- **Specialists**
	- like panda -> narrow niche
- **Generalists**
	- broad niche

- **Ecological niche**
	- the total of a species’ use of biotic and abiotic resources
		- also its ecological role
	- **Fundamental Niche** = ideal niche with no biotic intervention
	- **Realized Niche** = fundamental niche + the effect of biotic interactions
		- <= fundamental niche
---
## Biotic Interactions
- Competition
	- intraspecific
		- within species
	- interspecific
		- between species
- Predation
- Herbivory
- **Symbiotic**
	- Facilitation: at least one species benefits, no body get hurts
		- Mutualism +/+
		- Commensalism +/neutral
	- Parasitism +/- 
---
## Keystone Species
- a species that plays a role in its community that is far more important than its relative abundance might suggest
