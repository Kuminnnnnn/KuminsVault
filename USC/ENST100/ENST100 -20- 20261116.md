# Climate Change
- the weather conditions prevailing in an area in general or over a long period
---
## Controls on Earth's climate
- **4 Main Factors**
	- amount of sunlight received
	- albedo
	- retention of heat by the atmosphere
	- evaporation and condensation of water vapor
---
## What causes climate change
- solar input to earth's atmoshpere
	- *Milankovitch cycles*
		- eccentricity
		- obliquity
		- precession
	- suns luminocity
- distribution of continent
	- changes in albedo
	- changes in icesheet
	- changes in ocean current
- Volcanic eruptions  
	- Sulfate & Nitrate aerosols = cooling 
	- C aerosols and CO2 = warming 
- Atmospheric content of GHGs  
	- Higher GHG = warming
- CO<sub>2</sub>
	- Milankovitch -> initial temp change -> CO<sub>2</sub> release -> temp change

## Paleotemp&Paleoclimatology
- Proxies: preserved physical biological, geological or chemical materials from the past that stand in for direct meteorological measurements  
	- apply understanding of geochemistry, biology to past records 
	- Common proxies include:  
		- ice cores 
			- air in the bubbles
		- tree rings
			- dendrochronology
			- cliamte back ~9000 years
				- precipitation
				- fires
				- hurricane
				- temperature
		- corals 
		- ocean, lake sediments and microfossils

## GHG
- **Global warming potential (GWP)**: a measure of how much energy the emission of 1 ton of a gas will absorb over a given period of time, relative to the emission of 1ton of CO<sub>2</sub> 
- GHGs
	- water vapor
	- CO<sub>2</sub>
	- CH<sub>4</sub>
	- N<sub>2</sub>O
	- F-gas

## International Cliamte Science
- IPCCP: Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change  
	- Established by UN Environmental Program (UNEP) and World Meteorological Organization in 1988 
	- Over 3,000 scientist volunteers 
	- Reviews and assesses scientific, technical, and socio-economic info worldwide 
	- Intergovernmental; UN member countries participate in review process (195)  
	- Policy relevant but policy neutral
