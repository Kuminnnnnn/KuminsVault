# Environmental Studies
*The systematic study of human interaction with the environment*
- Highly interdisciplinary&integrative
	- bio, ecology, geology, sociology, geography...
- Identify problems and possible solutions to environment issues
- Sustainability
## Major Topics
- Climate change
- Eutrophication
- Biodiversity
- Pollutions
- Land use/deforestation
- Public health, environmental justice
- Policy solutions
## Environmental Systems
- Manmade or natural
- Ecosystem: a specific type of natural system
	- including biotic and abiotic
