# Climate and Biome
- **Climate**: the average weather in an area over a long period of time
	- plays the biggest role in determining the distribution of living communities
	- determined largely by **solar energy**
			- solar radiation on land is based on the planets movement in space
				- solar angle
				- surface area
				- distance
			- also affecting **convection**
				- movement caused by the tendency of hotter and therefore less dense air to rise, and colder, denser air to sink under
				- **adiabatic** cooling and warming effects lead to the cycle
				- **Three main Convection cell**
					- Polar
					- Ferel
					- Hadley
	- **Coriolis Effect**
		- Circulation + Coriolis effect = prevailing winds
			- winds help drive ocean circulation patters -> **Upwelling**
				- lower water temp
				- high nutrients
				- good fisheries
