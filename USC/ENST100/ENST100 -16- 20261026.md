# Renewable Energy
- The Sun is the ultimate source of almost all types of energy:
	- biomass
	- hydroelectricity
	- wind
	- solar
---
## What is Renewable Energy
1. Potentially renewable sources:
	1. can be replenished relatively quickly
	2. but, must not be consumed more quickly than replaced
2. Nondepletable sources:
	1. can't be depleted
	2. usage today will not deplete future
---
## Biomass
- **Most common type** of renewable energy
- replenishment depends on use and management
	- *Traditional*
		- world, charcoal, manure
	- *Biofuels*
		- ethanol, biodiesel
		- mostly used for transportation
- Pros:
	- potentially renewable, if properly managed
	- combustion still release CO<sub>2</sub>, but recent carbon not ancient carbon
- Cons:
	- Polluants
		- PM
		- CO
		- NO<sub>2</sub>
		- VOCs
	- Defoerstation
	- Agriculture pollution
## Hydroelectric
- 2nd most common
- driven by water kinetic energy
- **Types**
	- *Tidal*
		- gravitational pull from moon
	- *Water Impoundment*
		- solar and gravity
	- *Run-of-the-river systems*
		- water is held behind a low dam and runs through a channel to return to the river
		- do not store water to create reservoir
		- small
		- affected by drought /water flow
- Cons:
	- Impact of dams
		- reservoirs flood large land area
		- evaporation from reservoir
		- dams trap sediments, change natural hydrological regime -> impact up/downstream ecology
		- loss of natural river aesthetics
	- fish migration
	- tidal system may alter wetland dynamics
- Pros:
	- provides electricity
	- flood control
	- irrigation / drinking water
	- recreation
	- no air pollution
## Wind
- Pros:
	- lots of room for expansion
		- growth at 30% per yr, eventually account for 20% global demand
	- No air pollution
	- No water used
	- Turbines can be part of mixed landuse (cattle grazing)
- Cons:
	- can kill birds
	- use large land area
	- aesthetic
	- can be quite intermittent
## Solar
- *Passive solar energy*
	- can be used everywhere
- *Active solar energy*
	- use of pumps / solar PV / boiling water to generate electricity
	- Including:
		- Solar collector
		- Photovoltaics
		- Power Towers (Concentrating Solar Thermal)
- Pros:
	- relatively low impact on the environment
- Cons:
	- large land area is required
	- impact on wildlife
	- PV cells are expensive and have life span
	- requires longer sunlight days to decrease payback period
	- intermittent energy source
	- solar cell production need resources and may release chemical pollution
## Geothermal
- using the heat from deep within the Earth to provide power
- *Groud source heat pump*
	- takes advantage of high thermal inertia of ground
- Pros:
	- inexhaustible
	- constant/stable
	- small footprint
	- can also be used for heating
- Cons:
	- use groundwater
	- viable only in certain regions
	- some pollutants come out of ground
	- surface instability
	- expensive
## Hydrogen Fuel Cells
- Pros:
	- no pollution
	- fuel economy equivalent to 2x of gasoline vehicles
	- H is abundant and can be made from renewable energy
- Cons:
	- acceptable range requires extremely-high-pressure, on-board H storage
	- few place to refuel
	- H is expensive to transport
