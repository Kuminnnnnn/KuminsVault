# Nonrenewable Energy
## Common Units
- BTU =  British Thermal Unit
	- energy required to raise one pound of water by 1 degree F
- kWH = Kilowatt hours
	- a watt-hout is the energy one watt of power delivers for one hour
---
## Energy Use in the USA
### Transportation
- ~30% is for transportation
- public transit more efficient, but often less convenient
### Electricity
- ~40% is for generating electricity
- **Increase Efficiency**
	- Power plant
		- Co-generation -> use hot water to heat buildings
		- Combined cycle -> heat source turns multiple turbines
	- Transmission
		- Superconduction -> minimize energy loss
		- Smart grid ->smart appliances/meters that respond to local changes in demand/supply, incorporate power storage and renewables
- **Non-renewable**: Buried Sunlight
	- **Petroleum**
		- liquid and solid hydrocarbons
	- Natural Gas
	- **Coal**: Solid fuel formed primarily from remains of trees, ferns, and other plant materials that were preserved 280-360 million years ago.
		- Mining -> Mountain top removal
			- filling of valleys of sediments and removals
			- fragmented/removed forests
			- acidified water

	- Nuclear
