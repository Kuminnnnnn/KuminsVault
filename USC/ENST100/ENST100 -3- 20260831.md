### Matter in all ES
- anything that occupies space and has mass
	- atoms
		- smallest particle that contains the chemical properties of an element
	- elements
		- a substance that cannot be further divided, basic
	- molecules
#### Isotopes
- Isotope dating -> proxies of historical abiotic processes
- **Stable isotopes**: <sup>16</sup>O, <sup>17</sup>O, <sup>18</sup>O
- **Radioactive isotopes**: Uranium 238U and 234U in Ice cores, C14
- Trophic levels
- climate change C<sub>13</sub> to C<sub>12</sub>
- Dating via Half-lives
	- Decay of Carbon-14
	- application
		- mummies
		- ice/sediment cores
		- sediments
		- volcanic rocks
		- corals
### Chemical bonds
- **Ionic bonds:** forms by transferring electrons
- **Molecular compounds**
	- covalent bonds: sharing electron
	- hydrogen bonds: Due to molecule polarity, Hydrogen atoms that are covalently bonded to one atom are attracted to another atom on a different molecule.
---
### Organic and inorganic
- Inorganic: No C, or C is bonded to element other than H
- Organic: C-C or C-H
	- information: DNA
	- structure, transport, chemical reaction: DNA
	- Membrane, energy storage: lipid

### Cells
#### Water
- All cells and life needs water
- The hydrologic cycle
	- important for matter and energy movement
	- important for climate
- **Characteristics**
	- High melting and boiling point
	- high heat capacity
	- Universal solvent
	- has three states
	- less dense as a solid than a liquid
	- H bond result in higher freezing temp

### Energy
- the ability to do work
	- potential energy: energy that is stored
	- kinetic energy: energy of motion
- Power: rate at which work is done
#### Energy Units
**Joule** - fundamental energy unit
- energy or work done when a force of one newton is applied across 1 meter
- **Newton**: unit of force
