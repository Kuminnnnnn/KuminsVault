# What is Environmental Science
## Characteristics
- **Consistent**-does the phenomenon always occurs?
- **Observable**-can you experience or measure the phenomenon with your senses or technology
- **Natural**-natural cause or mechanism must be used to explain phenomenon, not supernatural
- **Predictable**
- **Testable**
- **Tentative**
## Two main approaches
- **Discovery**: mostly about describing nature
- **Hypothesis-driven**: mostly about explaining nature
## Science Jargon
- **Hypothesis:** an explanation for an observation that is capable of being disproved through experiments
- **Theory:** an explanation for a body of observations, based on evidence, repeatedly tested and widely accepted
- **Natural Law**: A theory that has been tested rigorously multiple times to which there are no known exceptions.
## Null and Alternative Hypothesis
- **H<sub>0</sub>** : The measured variable has no effect on the observed variable
- **H<sub>a</sub>** : The measured variable does have an effect on the observed variable
- **Results**: supporting of alternative hypothesis or ==Failing to Reject the Null Hypothesis==
## The Scientific Method
- Observations and questions
- Hypothesis
- Conduct Experiment/Collect Data
- Interpreting results
- Disseminating findings
## Experimentation
- **Laboratory experiments**
- **Field experiments**
- **Alternatives**
	- Natural events: extreme environments
	- Proxies: wood, stone, soil, ice
	- mathematical models
	- Meta Analysis

- difficult to decide whether one thing is better or worse for the environment than something else
