# light and noise pollution
- Pollute - to make unfit for or harmful to living things.  
- Pollutant - something that pollutes; a waste material that contaminates air, soil, or water. 
- Pollution - Contamination of air, soil, or water by the discharge of harmful substances.
---
## Light Pollution
- Excessive and prolonged use of artificial lighting a way that result in the brightening of night skies having unintended consequences
- **Three main types**
	- glare
	- light trespass
	- skyglow
- Nightscape has shifted from stars to city
- **History**
	- 300yrs ago -> lightning
	- 18th century -> cast iron fixtures
	- 19th century -> gas lamps
	- 20th century -> electric
- Outdoor lightning
	- provide safe and secure environment
	- extend the use of parks and walkways into the night
	- to enhance historic features
	- enhance travel on the roads and at intersactions
- **Negative Impacts**
	- astronomy
	- human health
		- circadian rhythms
		- production of melatonin
		- sleep disorders
	- waste of energy
	- disrupts ecosystems
		- altered physiology
		- increased predation and building strike
		- *Marine system*
			- zooplankton diel vertical migration suppression
			- bird strikes on lit ships at night
			- extension of visual foraging behavior in coastal wading birds into the night
			- Disruption of settlement site selection in sessile invertebrate larvae. 
			- Aggregation of fish under pier lights leads to intensified predation. 
			- De-synchronization of broadcast spawning from lunar phase (e.g. coral spawning)
			- Displacement of nesting sea turtles from artificially lit nesting areas.
			- Disorientation of migration in sea turtle hatchlings by street lights
- **Solutions**
	- shielding
	- timers, dimmers, motion sensors
	- wildlife conservation groups
	- regulations
- **Benefits of minimizing light pollution**
	- Decreases glare, light trespass, sky glow
	- Increases safety and security  
	- *Conserves energy* 
	- Respects the habits & habitats of animals & plants  
	- Maintains human health  
		- Circadian rhythm (can impact immunology.) 
		- Sleep disorders 
		- The aging or disabled eye  
		- Visibility (e.g., contrast)  
	- Provides good (nighttime) ambiance

## Noise pollution
- **Categories**
	- environmental
	- transportation
	- industrial
	- military recreation
	- other
- 70dB max threshold
- **Effects**
	- on humans
		- permanent hearing loss
		-  high blood pressure
		-  insomnia
		- labored breathing
		- stress
		- altered brain chemistry
		- increase in crime
		- permanent damage to the voice
- **Solutions**
	- compressor station
	- bubble curtain
	- speed limit
