## Ground water
- Two types of **aquifer**
	- *confined*: surrounded by a layer of impermeable rock or clay
	- *unconfined*: simply porous rock covered by soil
- **Overuse** -> 
	- **cone of depression**: an area where there is no longer any gourndwater
	- **subsidence** -> land sinking
	- **Salt water intrusion** -> cone of ascension
